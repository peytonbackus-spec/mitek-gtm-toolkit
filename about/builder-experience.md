# Where My Builder Experience Comes From

> Context: when I reached out about these roles, Nathan asked where my hands-on builder experience came from and what CRM building I've done. ## The short version

I came to GTM engineering from the SDR-leadership side. I built routing and scoring because my teams needed it, and Certa then made that the job: AI Systems & GTM Engineering, owning GTM systems and process across the full revenue org, from SDR through Customer Success.

## Timeline

| Role | Company | What I built / owned | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manager, Sales Development | Planful | Lead routing logic and account prioritization/scoring for the SDR team | Team of 12 SDRs including Team Leads; ~28 meetings/week in later stages |
| Head of Sales Development | Certa.ai (third-party risk management orchestration; Series B) | Routing logic, account scoring, SDR operating process | 5 direct-report SDRs; cross-functional oversight of 9 Sales Ops and 8 AEs; $3.5M target driven by ~45 qualified meetings/week (incl. expansion/renewals) |
| AI Systems & GTM Engineering | Certa.ai | GTM systems and process across the full revenue org, SDR → CS | Full revenue org |
| Founder | Independent AI GTM consulting practice | GTM audits, pipeline engines and RevOps builds for B2B SaaS | Delivery stack: Claude Code, Clay, HubSpot, n8n, Apollo |

Across roles: averaged ~120% of quota, and never missed target as an IC or a manager.
