## What Mitek sells

| Line | Products | Q3 FY26 |
|---|---|---|
| **Fraud & Identity** | MiVIP, IDLive Face/Doc, MiPass, Digital Fraud Defender | $29.0M (+14%); F&I SaaS $24.8M (+37%) |
| **Check Verification** | Mobile Deposit, Check Fraud Defender, Check Intelligence, Positive Pay Plus | $25.0M (+24%) |

Total Q3 FY26 revenue $54.0M (+18%). FY26 guidance raised to $195–200M. Fiscal year ends **Sep 30**, so Oct–Dec 2026 is FY27-Q1. About 80% of revenue comes from financial services.

## What changed in 2026

| Change | Why it matters for these roles |
|---|---|
| **Aaron Seyler joined as CRO (Aug 17, 2026)** and now runs sales, channel, CS, sales engineering and professional services as one org. Ex-Vonage and ex-Telesign (identity and fraud). | A new revenue leader merging five functions usually means a rebuild of routing, stages, forecast cadence and reporting, which is probably why both roles exist now |
| **Clari and Salesloft merged (Dec 2025)** | Two tools in Mitek's stack, one per posting, are now one platform |
| **Channel expanded** (Fiserv reseller, Abrigo, CSI, Data Advisor) | Partner-sourced pipeline needs its own workflow and attribution |
| **Top-five US bank joined the Check Fraud Defender consortium** | Enterprise bank motion and network effects |
| **SaaS share rising** (~46% of TTM revenue) | Renewals and usage telemetry matter more every quarter |
